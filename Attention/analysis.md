# Analysis

## Layer 4, Head 6

There is a clear connection between each word and the word before it.

Example Sentences:
- Pigs and [MASK] are both dirty animals
- Lettuce and [MASK] are both vegetables

## Layer 5, Head 6

The linking verb has a greater attention towards its identifier noun.
In these cases, "are" as the linking verb and "animals" and "vegetables" as the identifier nouns.

Example Sentences:
- Pigs and [MASK] are both dirty animals
- Lettuce and [MASK] are both vegetables
